THE MASTER CHECKLIST

15 steps to protect your claim.

Work through each step from unboxing to claim resolution. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser. Check off items as you complete them.

PHASE 01

Before Anything Goes Wrong

0/4 DONE
STEP 01 PRE-FAILURE RECORD

Keep the invoice.

Save the original bill and a photo or PDF copy. The warranty normally starts on the invoice date.

Action: Upload a PDF scan to a designated cloud folder named "Warranty_Records".
STEP 02 PRODUCT ACTIVATION

Register the product.

Many brands ask you to register on their website or app; some add extra warranty when you do.

Action: Screenshot registration confirmation screen showing serial number and end date.
STEP 03 TERMS REVIEW

Read the warranty card once.

Note how long it lasts, what it covers, and what it excludes.

Action: Highlight exclusions (e.g. power surge, accidental drops, unauthorized service).
STEP 04 INITIAL BASELINE

Record the condition.

Take a few dated photos of the product once it is installed or unboxed.

Action: Photograph the serial number label, display panel, casing, and power cable.
PHASE 02

When The Fault Appears

0/3 DONE
STEP 05 EVIDENCE CAPTURE

Record the fault.

Take a clear photo or short video of the problem as soon as you see it, with the date visible if you can.

Action: Ensure camera date stamp or calendar/clock on screen is clearly captured.
STEP 06 COMMUNICATION TRAIL

Raise the complaint in writing.

Use email, the brand's website or its app, not only a phone call, so there is a record.

Action: Use Template A below. Phone promises cannot be verified in consumer tribunals.
STEP 07 LOGGING SYSTEM

Note every reference number.

Write down the complaint or ticket number, the date and the name of anyone you speak to.

Action: Maintain a dedicated notes document: Date • Time • Representative Name • Reference #.
PHASE 03

When A Technician Visits

0/3 DONE
STEP 08 INSPECTION REPORT

Ask for the job sheet.

Ask the technician for a copy of the visit report or job sheet before they leave.

Action: Photograph the paper job sheet or ensure an electronic copy arrives before departure.
STEP 09 TECHNICAL PROOF

Ask what was tested.

Ask which test or instrument was used to reach the diagnosis.

Action: Ask: "What multimeter reading or diagnostic tool log indicated this component failed?"
STEP 10 ON-SITE DOCUMENTATION

Take your own photos.

Photograph the product and anything the technician points to, during the visit.

Action: Photograph internal boards, warranty seals, and technician diagnostic screens.
PHASE 04

If The Claim Is Refused

0/5 DONE
STEP 11 FORMAL JUSTIFICATION

Ask for the reason in writing.

Request a written explanation that names the exact cause and the evidence for it.

Action: Reject phone-call denials. Require written citation of specific contract clause.
STEP 12 EVIDENCE DEMAND

Ask for the technical report.

If the refusal rests on an inspection, ask for that inspection report.

Action: As the product owner, you are legally entitled to receive the technical inspection dossier.
STEP 13 INTERNAL DISPUTE

Escalate inside the company.

Most brands list a grievance or escalation contact on their website. Write to it, quoting your complaint number.

Action: Search for "Nodal Officer", "Appellate Authority" or "Head of Customer Care".
STEP 14 LEGAL READINESS

Keep it factual.

Stick to dates, documents and photos. Calm, written records are what a consumer forum or regulator will look at.

Action: Avoid emotional language. State facts chronologically: Date, Action, Discrepancy.
STEP 15 STATUTORY REDRESS

Know your consumer helpline.

Most countries run a free consumer helpline or online complaint portal that can guide you on the next step.

Action: Refer to the official government helplines directory listed below.
EVIDENCE & CORRESPONDENCE

Written records make claims indisputable.

Copy and adapt these standard factual notices. Send them via email or brand support portals to establish an undeniable audit trail.

CORRESPONDENCE TEMPLATE FOR STEP 06

Initial Written Complaint Notice

Subject: Formal Warranty Service Request — [Product Model] — [Serial Number] Dear Customer Support, I purchased [Product Brand & Model] on [Purchase Date] (Invoice Ref: [Invoice Number]). On [Date Fault Appears], the product developed the following issue: [Describe fault accurately and concisely: e.g., display panel showing vertical line defect]. As this unit is within its active warranty period ending [Warranty End Date], I request an official service appointment or replacement in accordance with warranty terms. Please confirm this request with a formal ticket/complaint reference number. Attached: 1. Proof of Purchase (Invoice) 2. Photo/Video evidence of fault with date stamp
Format: Email / Support Web Ticket
INSPECTION RECORD FOR STEPS 08 & 09

Technician Job Sheet & Diagnostic Proof

Formal Request for On-Site Inspection Report & Diagnostic Logs Complaint Reference: [Ticket Number] Technician Name: [Name of Visiting Engineer] Visit Date: [Date] In respect of today's service visit for [Product & Serial Number]: 1. Please provide an immediate duplicate copy or digital transmission of Job Sheet / Field Service Report #[Report Number]. 2. Please record the specific diagnostic test, hardware inspection instrument, or firmware tool used to reach the diagnosis. 3. State whether the defect is determined to be manufacturing/hardware origin or external cause. Customer Signature: __________________ Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Format: In-Person Job Sheet Endorsement
REJECTION APPEAL FOR STEPS 11 & 12

Demand for Written Refusal & Technical Report

Subject: Request for Written Basis of Warranty Claim Refusal — Ticket #[Ticket Number] Dear Claims Department, Regarding the verbal refusal of warranty service communicated on [Date]: In accordance with consumer protection provisions and standard contract law, I hereby formally request: 1. A written statement specifying the exact warranty clause or condition invoked to refuse coverage. 2. The complete technical inspection report, including all diagnostic readings, instrument results, and dated photographs upon which this decision was made. Please provide this documentation in writing within 5 working days so that it may be placed on formal record.
Format: Formal Written Notice
ESCALATION RECORD FOR STEP 13

Internal Grievance & Executive Escalation

To: Grievance Officer / Appellate Authority / Customer Relations Head Company: [Brand Name] Subject: Formal Grievance Escalation — Complaint #[Ticket Number] — Unresolved Warranty Claim Dear Grievance Officer, I am writing to escalate an unresolved warranty issue regarding [Product Name & Serial Number]. Chronology of Facts: - [Date 1]: Product purchased (Invoice #[Number]) - [Date 2]: Fault occurred and reported via Ticket #[Number] - [Date 3]: Technician inspection conducted (Job Sheet #[Number]) - [Date 4]: Claim rejected without substantiated technical justification The product remains under full manufacturer warranty. I request your review of this matter and resolution (repair/replacement) within 7 business days, failing which this dossier will be submitted to the statutory consumer forum.
Format: Executive Grievance Dossier
STEP 15 REFERENCE DIRECTORY

Statutory consumer helplines & portals.

Most countries operate free official consumer helplines, dispute tribunals, and online mediation portals. If the company stalls, file your chronological dossier here.

MALAYSIA • KPDN

Tribunal Tuntutan Pengguna (TTPM)

Free consumer hotline and formal e-Tribunal for resolving electronic & appliance warranty disputes without expensive lawyer fees.

Portal: ttpm.kpdn.gov.my • 1-800-886-800 →
UNITED STATES • FTC

Federal Trade Commission (FTC)

Reports Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act violations and deceptive denial practices. State Attorney General consumer divisions accept formal dispute complaints.

Portal: reportfraud.ftc.gov →
UNITED KINGDOM • CITIZENS ADVICE

Citizens Advice Consumer Service

Statutory assistance under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Advises on goods that are not of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, or as described.

Portal: citizensadvice.org.uk • 0808 223 1133 →
INDIA • DOCA

National Consumer Helpline (NCH / e-Daakhil)

Toll-free 1915 helpline and e-Daakhil electronic consumer court filing platform under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

Portal: consumerhelpline.gov.in • Dial 1915 →
AUSTRALIA • ACCC

Australian Consumer Law (ACCC)

Consumer Guarantees apply automatically under Australian Consumer Law (ACL), regardless of manufacturer voluntary warranty limits.

Portal: accc.gov.au/consumers →
GLOBAL • CONSUMERS INTERNATIONAL

International Consumer Redress

Federation of 200+ consumer organizations across 100+ countries providing country-specific regulatory guides and arbitration pathways.

Portal: consumersinternational.org →
